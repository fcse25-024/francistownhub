About Francistown Business Hub

A community-driven platform built to connect Francistown residents with the micro and macro businesses that serve them.

Our Mission

Connecting people with the businesses that serve them

Francistown is Botswana's second largest city — a vibrant commercial hub with a rich mix of local entrepreneurs, established brands, and essential services. Yet, finding reliable information about local businesses has always been a challenge.

This hub was created to solve that problem. We believe every business in Francistown — from the corner general dealer to the flagship hotel — deserves visibility, and every resident deserves easy access to the services they need.
